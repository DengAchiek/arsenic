Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and support.

What solar panel size do I need?

It depends on your daily energy usage. Use our solar calculator for a rough estimate, or contact us for a site-specific recommendation.

Do you offer installation services?

We focus on supplying quality products. We can connect you with trusted installation partners in your area just ask.

What's your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Opened items may be accepted for exchange or store credit if they're in resalable condition. Contact support for details.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders are processed within 2 business days. Delivery times vary by location typically 2-7 days within Kenya.

Do you offer warranties on products?

Yes. Warranty periods vary by product type and are listed on each product page. All warranties are manufacturer-backed.
